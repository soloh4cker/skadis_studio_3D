Print your first shelf

  1. Check the hooks first. The small test uses your selected hook type and board spacing. Two-row mode tests both rows. Insert the tails, then slide down to seat them. Measure the slot pattern and adjust the fit if needed.
  2. Download your shelf. 3MF keeps the model's millimeter units. If your app does not accept the 3MF, use STL and import it in millimeters.
  3. Slice for your printer. Select your printer and filament. A starting point is 0.20 mm layers, 4 walls, and 20–30% infill. Inspect the sliced preview and add removable supports where needed.
  4. Check the print orientation. Structural brackets underneath belong to your shelf. They can leave the shelf floor above the build plate and require substantial slicer supports. You can rotate the model onto a side or back in your slicer, then inspect hook and wall overhangs. The tool does not choose the best print orientation.
  5. Test before loading it. Check that every hook fully seats and the board is secured to the wall. Test a small load first. More hooks and brackets do not establish a weight rating.

Exports put the lowest point at Z = 0. Blue is the preview color; choose your actual filament in the slicer. No multicolor setup or prime tower is required.
